Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy covers DrySafe ("we", "us", "our"), the trading name of Ekon Enterprises Pty Ltd (ACN 061 879 141), trading as DrySafe Response Group. It applies to our websites, including drysafe.sydney and mould.drysafe.sydney, and to the water damage and mould services we provide in Sydney. It explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, and how you can access or correct it. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Information we collect

  • Contact and booking details you give us when you book, request a quote or contact us: your name, email address, mobile number, service address and details of the service you need.
  • Mould Action Plan answers. If you ask for a Mould Action Plan, we collect your answers about your property (for example, what you've noticed, which rooms and surfaces are affected, any water damage, and whether you rent, own or manage the property), your name, email, mobile and postcode, and the date and time you answered.
  • Advertising form details. If you respond to one of our ads on Facebook or Instagram, Meta passes us the details you enter in its form, such as your name, email and answers, and whether you ticked any consent box.
  • Payment information, processed by our payment provider, Stripe. We don't store your full card details.
  • Usage, analytics and advertising data, such as pages visited, actions taken, device and browser details, and identifiers from ads you clicked (for example Meta and Google click identifiers and cookies).
  • Messages and calls with us, including texts you reply with, such as STOP.

How we use your information

  • To create and send the Mould Action Plan you ask for, by email and text message, and to keep a record of it.
  • To contact you about your plan, your enquiry or your booking by email, SMS or phone, including to offer a call with a mould expert or a booking.
  • To provide, schedule and invoice the services you book, and to process payments.
  • If you opt in, to send you occasional mould tips and offers.
  • To measure and improve our websites and advertising, including telling advertising platforms (such as Meta) that an enquiry or booking happened, in a form those platforms match without receiving your details in readable form (we send hashed identifiers).
  • To meet our legal obligations.

A Mould Action Plan is general information based on the answers you give. It is not advice for your property.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only as needed to run our business. We use these service providers:

  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google, for advertising and measurement.
  • Supabase and Vercel, which host our databases and websites.
  • Amazon Web Services (S3), which stores plan PDFs and photos.
  • Resend, which sends our emails, and Twilio, which sends our text messages.
  • Notion and Slack, which our team uses to manage enquiries.
  • PostHog, for website analytics.
  • Stripe, for payments, and Cal.com, if you book a call online.

We may also share information with our technicians and contractors who carry out work you book, and with authorities where the law requires it. We don't sell your personal information.

Overseas disclosure

Several of these providers store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States and other countries where they operate. We choose providers with recognised security practices and share only what each one needs.

Marketing and opting out

You can opt out of emails and texts from us at any time: use the unsubscribe link in any email, reply STOP to any text, or contact us using the details below. We act on opt-outs within five working days.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, including access controls and encrypted connections. We keep plans, enquiries and booking records for as long as we need them to deal with you, provide our services and meet our legal obligations.

Cookies and tracking

Our websites use cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel, Google tags and PostHog, for analytics and advertising measurement. You can control cookies through your browser settings; turning them off may affect some features.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask for access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you by contacting us. If you have a concern about how we've handled your information, contact us first and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
