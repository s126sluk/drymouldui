STEP INSIDE A DRYSAFE JOB

Here's what happens in your home.

Five steps, from the first look to the report in your inbox.

STEP 01 / 05REMEDIATION PROGRESS 20%

Assess.

Every job starts with a visual mould assessment and moisture and humidity readings. Mould needs moisture, so we take readings and understand where the moisture could potentially be to help you take the right steps to prevent it from reoccurring.

WHAT YOU GET FROM STEP ONE

  • ✓ A visual mould assessment of each room you've booked
  • ✓ Moisture and humidity readings, recorded on the day
  • ✓ Photos of the affected surfaces before anything is touched
  • ✓ A plain-English explanation of what we found

Smells musty but can't see anything? That smell is worth taking seriously. It usually comes from growth somewhere you can't see, which is why we take readings rather than just looking.

Prepare.

YOUR PART: VERY LITTLE

01 · DON'T SCRUB ITPlease don't scrub, spray or wipe the area. It makes it harder to see what we're dealing with and can spread the problem.
02 · CLOSE THE ROOMClose the room off if you reasonably can, and keep it that way until we arrive.
03 · STOP ACTIVE WATERIf there's a live leak, get a plumber to it first. That's a separate trade to ours.
04 · CLEAR ACCESSMove small furniture, valuables and anything breakable if it's safe to.
05 · TAKE PHOTOSPhotograph the area as it is now and note roughly when you first saw it. Useful for your own records.
06 · TELL US ABOUT THE HOUSEHOLDPets, small children and access details like parking, stairs, lift bookings or strata rules.
