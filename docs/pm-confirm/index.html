2. SERVICE ACTIVITIES & DEFINITIONS

The following table outlines the activities that may be performed during your service:

Visual Mould AssessmentA visual assessment of mould presence on wall surfaces in the room. Only areas that are visible and not covered by furniture / contents items are assessed. Furniture and contents will not be relocated or shifted.
Surface HEPA Vacuum (Walls Only)HEPA vacuum of wall surfaces only. Will only be performed on areas that are visible and not covered by furniture / contents items. Floors, ceilings and contents items are excluded unless expressly included by your selected package.
Anti-microbial ApplicationSurface application to the surfaces included in your selected package (wall surfaces; and where the HomeProtect upgrade is selected, window sills, door / window tracks and ceiling surfaces). Will only be performed on areas that are visible and not covered by clothing/furniture/contents items. If there are specific concerns or further details required regarding the anti-microbial product, it is the responsibility of the customer or resident to obtain this information from the DrySafe Response Group office and to raise any concerns with the attending technician prior to application. Unless such enquiries are made and addressed beforehand, the application will proceed as part of the approved works included in the customer's selected package.
Furniture Protection / EncapsulationFurniture and contents items within the work area are protected / encapsulated with plastic sheeting prior to works commencing. Items will not be relocated or shifted.
Furniture Adjustment (Max 15kg)Adjustment / repositioning of furniture items less than 15kg to enable access to affected wall surfaces. Items over 15kg will not be lifted or moved.
Anti-microbial + HEPA Vacuum — Window Sills & Door / Window TracksApplication of anti-microbial and HEPA vacuum to window sills and door / window tracks. Will only be performed on areas that are visible and accessible.
Anti-microbial + HEPA Vacuum — Ceiling SurfacesApplication of anti-microbial and HEPA vacuum to ceiling surfaces inside the room. Will only be performed on areas that are safely reachable.
Air Filtration Device (AFD) OperationOne air filtration device operated in the room during the works only (minimum 15 minutes, maximum 2 hours of operation). The device must have adequate space around its perimeter and is removed on completion of the visit; no equipment remains on site.
Dehumidification OperationOne dehumidifier operated in the room during the works only (minimum 15 minutes, maximum 2 hours of operation). The unit is removed on completion of the visit; no equipment remains on site.
Thermal Imaging5x thermal images per room including one image per wall (four walls total) as well as an image of the ceiling inside that room.
Moisture ReportOne surface reading of either skirting, wall lining or subfloor. One relative humidity reading inside the room, captured during the visit.
Works Report & ScopeBefore/after images will be captured by room and a full activity list of what works were performed will also be included, together with a scope of any recommended further works.
